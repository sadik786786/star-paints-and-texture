Business Overview

Engineered for Indian climates, trusted by architects & builders

Star Paints & Texture is a dedicated manufacturer of high-performance wall finishes, including decorative textures, industrial primers, premium distemper, and exterior emulsions. Our products are designed for both interior and exterior applications, combining aesthetic appeal with long-lasting protection.

Our manufacturing facility spans over 50,000 sq.ft., equipped with automated production lines and a dedicated quality control lab. Every batch undergoes rigorous testing for weather resistance, adhesion, and color consistency.
